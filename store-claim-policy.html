Store Claim Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Overview

At SneakerHub, we stand behind the quality of our products. This Store Claim Policy outlines your rights and our procedures for returns, exchanges, refunds, and warranty claims. Please read this policy carefully before making a purchase.

2. 30-Day Return Policy

We offer a 30-day return window from the date of delivery for all eligible items.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Items must be unworn, unused, and in original condition
  • All original tags, labels, and packaging must be intact
  • Items must be returned in the original shoebox with all accessories
  • Proof of purchase (order number) required

Non-Returnable Items:

  • Worn or damaged items
  • Items missing original packaging or tags
  • Custom or personalized orders
  • Clearance/final sale items (marked as such)
  • Gift cards

3. Return Process

  1. Initiate Return: Contact our support team at support@sneakerhub.com with your order number and reason for return
  2. Receive Authorization: We will provide a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and return shipping label
  3. Package Items: Place items in original packaging with all accessories and include the RMA number
  4. Ship Return: Drop off at authorized carrier location
  5. Inspection: Returns are inspected within 3-5 business days of receipt
  6. Refund: Approved refunds processed within 5-10 business days to original payment method

4. Exchanges

We offer exchanges for size or color variations when available.

  • Exchanges must be requested within 30 days of delivery
  • Exchanges are subject to inventory availability
  • Price differences will be charged or refunded accordingly
  • Exchange shipping is free for the first exchange per order

5. Refund Policy

Full Refunds:

  • Items returned within 30 days meeting all eligibility criteria
  • Defective or damaged items (reported within 7 days of delivery)
  • Incorrect items shipped by SneakerHub

Partial Refunds:

  • Items returned without original packaging: 20% restocking fee
  • Items with minor wear: Up to 50% refund at our discretion

Non-Refundable:

  • Original shipping charges (unless error on our part)
  • Gift wrap or special packaging fees

6. Defective Products & Warranty

All products come with a manufacturer's warranty against defects in materials and workmanship.

Manufacturing Defects Include:

  • Sole separation from upper
  • Stitching coming undone under normal wear
  • Material defects (cracking, peeling, discoloration not due to wear)
  • Structural failures

Not Covered:

  • Normal wear and tear
  • Damage from improper use, accidents, or neglect
  • Modifications or repairs by unauthorized parties
  • Improper cleaning or storage
  • Fit or comfort preferences

7. Warranty Claim Process

  1. Contact support with order number, photos of defect, and description
  2. Our team will assess within 2-3 business days
  3. Approved claims: We provide prepaid return shipping label
  4. Options: Repair, replacement, or refund (at our discretion)
  5. Replacements shipped within 5 business days of receiving return

8. Damaged or Lost Shipments

If your order arrives damaged or is lost in transit:

  • Report within 48 hours of delivery (or expected delivery date)
  • Provide photos of damaged packaging and items
  • We will file a claim with the carrier and send a replacement at no cost
  • Do not discard original packaging until claim is resolved

9. Incorrect Orders

If you receive the wrong item:

  • Contact us within 7 days with photos
  • We will send the correct item with prepaid return label for incorrect item
  • No cost to you for the exchange

10. Claim Denials

Claims may be denied if:

  • Item shows signs of wear beyond normal try-on
  • Damage caused by misuse, neglect, or unauthorized modifications
  • Claim submitted outside applicable timeframes
  • Item purchased from unauthorized reseller
  • Insufficient documentation provided
